THREE STEPS TO YOUR NEXT WORLD

Get set up.
Then get playing.

No complicated tour. Install Eclipse, choose how you play, and bring your preferred content into the setup.

Install Eclipse

Download the Windows x64 setup wizard and follow the installation steps. The packaged launcher includes its runtime.

02 / PROFILE◈

Choose your setup

Sign in with Microsoft or create a local profile for supported offline and singleplayer use. Select Vanilla or Fabric.

03 / PERSONALISE✳

Make it yours

Add compatible content, tune settings and launch your selected profile.
